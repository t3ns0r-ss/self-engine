## Contradicts his note

- Euler's theorem. Old "What this topic does not cover": "Euler's theorem for a composite modulus ... not part of this course". His note has the section "Euler's Theorem: (General version)." with the example 3^100 mod 10 and the generalised form a^(n mod phi(m) + phi(m)). The old scope line must not be copied.
- When an inverse exists. Old Theorem 2.2.3: "a has an inverse modulo m exactly when gcd(a, m) = 1". His note, "Modular inverse": "Modulo inverses are only possible for prime moduli." The note then adds "the exact condition is gcd(a, m) = 1" with m = 10 as the example, so the star line is loose and the note corrects itself.
- Multiplication for large moduli. Old Theorem 2.2.1 needs: "For m up to 10^18, multiply in __int128". His note, "Binary multiplication": add-and-double, safe while M is at most about 4.6x10^18, and __int128 only as an aside. Different convention, same result.
