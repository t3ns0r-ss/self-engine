## cses-1671 Shortest Routes I (tier 2, pattern dijkstra, set reserved, for lookalike)
- Budget: n ≤ 10^5, m ≤ 2·10^5, weights ≤ 10^9.
- Idea: Dijkstra from city 1 with a binary heap and lazy deletion (skip popped entries whose distance is stale); 64-bit distances.
- Techniques: 4.3
- Needs this topic because: look-alike partner of Shortest Routes II: one source and a big sparse graph, so Dijkstra, not Floyd–Warshall.
- Tier reasoning: early CSES graph problem.
