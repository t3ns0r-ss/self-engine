---
title: CP Training
description: A self-study programme from beginner to Codeforces Expert, with an emphasis on recognising which technique a problem needs.
---

This site takes you from your first competitive programming problem to roughly Codeforces Expert level.
Lessons teach one technique at a time and tie each technique to the property that makes it correct,
so you learn to recognise when it applies.

The lessons are being written. The [problem bank](/bank/) lists the practice problems for every topic,
sorted by difficulty tier, and you can log your attempts there now.
