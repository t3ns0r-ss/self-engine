## lc-two-city-scheduling Two City Scheduling (tier 3, pattern exchange-order, set practice)
- Budget: 2n ≤ 100.
- Idea: Sort people by aCost − bCost; send the first n to city A and the rest to B. Swapping two people in the wrong order changes the total by the difference of their keys, so this order is optimal.
- Techniques: 1.5, 1.1
- Needs this topic because: an exchange argument fixes the sort key.
- Tier reasoning: LeetCode Medium.
