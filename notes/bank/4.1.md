## cses-1192 Counting Rooms (tier 1, pattern grid-graph, set practice)
- Budget: n, m ≤ 1000.
- Idea: Scan every floor cell; each unvisited one starts a BFS/DFS that marks its room. The number of starts is the answer. Use an explicit stack or BFS (recursion depth can reach 10^6).
- Techniques: 4.1
- Needs this topic because: flood fill on a grid.
- Tier reasoning: first problem of the CSES graph section.
