This exam is deliberately mixed. Every problem below is about strings, about pieces of a text, their beginnings, their endings or their order, and that is all the statements share: in one problem the pieces are compared for equality, in one a pattern is looked for in a long text, in one a set of words shares beginnings, and in one the substrings are ordered or counted. The 6 problems come from all four Phase 6 topics, in an order that has nothing to do with the course, and nothing on the page names their topic until you have finished. The question is always the same: **what is compared in this string, and which tool makes that comparison cheap?**

**How to take it.**

1. Set aside 4 hours without interruptions. The timer keeps running if you reload the page.
2. Read every problem first, then solve them in the order you prefer. Use only what you would use in a contest: your own code and the C++ reference.
3. For each problem, note the first tool you tried. If it was the wrong one, that is a *Recognition miss*, even if you later solved the problem.
4. When the time is up (or you are done), mark what you solved within the time and where you had a Recognition miss.

**Pass rule.** At least 60% of the problems solved in time (4 of 6), and no Recognition miss on a solved problem. If you do not pass, the remediation map lists the topic, card and lesson section behind each problem: reread those, upsolve the problems, and sit the exam again after a few days.
