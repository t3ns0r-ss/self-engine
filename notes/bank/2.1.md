## lc-number-of-different-subsequences-gcds Number of Different Subsequences GCDs (tier 4, pattern multiples-sieve, set reserved, for lookalike)
- Budget: n ≤ 10^5, values ≤ 2·10^5.
- Idea: Mark the present values. For each g ≤ max, take the GCD of all present multiples of g. g is the GCD of some subsequence exactly when that GCD equals g: every subsequence with GCD g consists of multiples of g, and adding the other present multiples keeps the GCD a multiple of g that divides g. Harmonic loop, O(M log M · log M).
- Techniques: 2.1, 0.4
- Needs this topic because: the answer for g depends on its multiples; looping over them for every g is the harmonic bound.
- Tier reasoning: LeetCode Hard.
